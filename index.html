About PASS Car Wash

Hands-On Precision
In Bethel, Addis Ababa

PASS Car Wash House (@pass.car.wash) is a specialized automotive cleaning shop situated in Bethel, right in front of Al-Baik. We focus on rigorous interior detailing, engine compartment restoration, wheel degreasing, and total vehicle shines.

Demonstrated across tens of thousands of video views on TikTok, our team utilizes step-by-step hands-on methods to transform dirty vehicles, restore dark engine bays, and revive interior upholstery.
